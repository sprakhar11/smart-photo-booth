Frame Change

Blink to repaint reality

  1. Hold up both hands, thumb and index spread into an L. The top edge joins your two index tips, the bottom edge your two thumbs.
  2. Close your eyes for a moment to change the colour, view and light inside the frame. A normal blink is ignored, so hold it just briefly.
  3. Gather all four tips together to start recording, and again to stop. A download button appears when you're done.

Camera stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
