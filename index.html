Frame Change

Pinch to repaint reality

  1. Hold up both hands and spread thumb and index into an L shape. Those four tips are your corners.
  2. They build a live quadrilateral that tracks you smoothly — move your hands and the frame follows.
  3. Touch thumb to index on either hand to change the colour, view and light inside the frame. The frame holds its shape while you tap.

Camera stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
